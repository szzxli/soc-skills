# Feedback → Product

How real discussions shaped soc-skills. Every principle below came from someone
pushing back.

## 2026-10-16 · r/SecurityCareerAdvice (hiring manager, 10 years of data)

- **"10 years, 1 recruiter clicked"** (Anxious_Alps_4150)
  → Distribution corrected: link-on-resume is the failure mode. The 30-second
  summary must be readable without clicking; employer-initiated is the primary
  channel.
- **"Hiring 1 person to do 3–4 jobs"**
  → Multi-hat readiness matrix (Phase 4 core component).
- **Budget cuts across cyber teams**
  → Employer-side cost iron rule: near-zero cost or death.
- **"Appsec at Meta" and the 6-second scan**
  → 6-second one-liner layer; funnel model (6s → 30s → matrix → evidence chain).
- **"Another framework for the HM to learn"**
  → Framework invisibility principle; north star: become the GPA of hands-on
  security skill.
- **"The market has passed the juniors by"**
  → Honest positioning: the product doesn't create demand, it lowers the cost
  of finding signal; profiles serve seniors too.

## 2026-10-16 · r/SecurityCareerAdvice ("line cook" critique, jhkoenig)

- **"A trial shift, not a resume"**
  → Mid-task injects in advanced lab cards: priority changes, alert interrupts,
  tool outages mid-task.

## 2026-10-16 · Founder thesis (from the above debates)

- **"Years of experience is technically unquantifiable"**
  → Tenure measures the past (poorly); learning velocity predicts the future.
  Added to thesis + Phase 4 learning-velocity tracking.
- **"Free, portable, candidate-owned — but HR must recognize it"**
  → Recognition is earned through predictive validity, not bought. Outcome
  tracking loop: profile → interview → hire → 6-month performance.
